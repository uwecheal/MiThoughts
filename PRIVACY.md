# MiThoughts Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 19 November 2026_

MiThoughts is a mind-mapping app for the Mac. It does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **No data collection.** MiThoughts has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send your maps or any other information to the developer or to anyone else.
- **Your files stay on your Mac.** Maps are saved only where you choose to save them. If you keep them in iCloud Drive or another synced folder, that service's own privacy terms apply.
- **Reminders and Calendar.** If you choose to send a task to Apple Reminders or an event to Apple Calendar, MiThoughts asks for your permission first and only writes the items you create. This data stays in Apple's apps on your devices.
- **Crash recovery.** To protect unsaved work, MiThoughts keeps a copy of the open map inside its own app container on your Mac.
